Your whole game library, built for the couch.

Technetium is a full-screen game launcher for Windows. It finds your Steam and Epic games, works with Xbox 360 and DualShock 4 controllers, and stays out of the way once you press play.

Everything in one row.

Pick up a controller, press a button, and play. No tiny menus and no mouse hunting.

Finds your games for you

Technetium reads your Steam libraries on every drive and your Epic Games installs. Anything else, you add by choosing its .exe.

Made for controllers

Xbox 360 and DualShock 4 controllers work without setup, and the button hints change to match yours. Mouse and keyboard work just as well.

Updates Steam games without Steam

Check for updates from Settings. Games with a newer version get a badge, and one more press installs them with a progress bar. It uses SteamCMD, Valve’s own tool.

Updates itself

Settings has a Check for updates button, and Technetium also looks quietly each time it starts.

Opens when you turn on your PC

Switch on Start with Windows in Settings and your library is waiting after you sign in.

Comfortable to look at

Soft blues, rounded covers, and slow, gentle motion. The layout scales from a big TV down to a small window.

Install it in a minute

  1. Download the installerGet Technetium Setup from the Releases page.
  2. Run itWindows may warn that the app is unrecognized because it isn’t code-signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open TechnetiumYour Steam and Epic games appear on their own. Use Add game for the rest.
  4. Optional: turn on updatesIn Settings, choose Check game updates. The first time, a console window asks you to sign in to Steam once.

Questions

Does it replace Steam?

No. Steam still runs your games and handles online play and achievements. Technetium is the screen you use to choose and start them.

Is updating games through Technetium safe?

It uses SteamCMD, Valve’s official command-line tool, and your own Steam account. You type your password into SteamCMD’s own window, never into Technetium. Close Steam while updating, and Steam may do a quick file check on that game afterwards.

Which games does it find?

Steam games on any drive and Epic Games installs. Games from other stores, including the Xbox app, can be added by choosing their .exe with Add game.

My controller isn’t detected.

Press any button on it once. If you use Steam Input, try turning it off for the desktop, because Steam can take control of the controller.
